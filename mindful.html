Pretzel Relaxation

Pretzel Steps:

  1. Set a timer for 1-5 minutes. It can be helpful to use music as your timer (set it to turn off when the time is up)
  2. Cross your right arm over your left arm (palms facing, thumbs down)
  3. Clasp your fingers together, and turn your hands so they are crossed against your chest
  4. Sit with your legs in a criss cross
  5. Breathe until the time is up
  6. Repeat, but with the left arm over the right one
