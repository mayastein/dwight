CLEAN

The genre of songs in this playlist is a bit all over the place, but there is a common theme! These are songs that I feel relate to mental health recovery journeys. When you listen to the songs, try following along with the lyrics. You might find something you can relate/connect to!

FUEL

This is a great playlist to use to enter a calm headspace. I find that, when I am upset, the last thing I want to do is listen to happy/upbeat music. I need music that is able to fit my mood. However, sad music is not always the best idea when dysregulated. I created this playlist to serve as a balance: the songs are not overly upbeat, but they also aren't overwhelmingly depressing.

3 SONGS

This is my playlist for when I enter into CRISIS MODE. It is a small collection of peaceful music that tends to calm me down. The playlist is called 3 songs because the challenge is to pause and play 3 songs before proceeding. You can use this playlist like a timer!

NO CHAMPAGNE JUST PROBLEMS

Take your emotional temperature before listening to this playlist. This is a sad playlist, because sometimes you just need to play some music and cry it out. Be sure to use caution, however, because sad music has the potential to intensify a crisis and make your feelings too intense.
