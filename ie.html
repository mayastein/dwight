Interpersonal Effectiveness Skills

DEARMAN

DEARMAN is a skill to help you ask for what you want/need or say no to an unreasonable request. The acronym creates a template for writing scripts that can help you prepare for difficult conversations.

Check out this resource to learn more about the DEARMAN Skill

Social Supports

Social connections are SO IMPORTANT! Humans are a social species. We (literally) need connection to survive.

Information about social connection:
  • You likely have different people that you encounter in different areas of your life.
  • For example, you probably talk to your boss about work-related issues and not a personal emotional crisis. Additionally, the people you confide in for personal matters likely do not know all the details/processes that you discuss with your boss.
  • It can be helpful to reflect on the people you have in your life, and who may be beneficial to contact when you need support in different areas of your life
Worksheets for social support education and identification:
  • Therapist Aid Worksheet
  • National Center for PTSD Worksheet
  • Wisc.edu Worksheet

Boundaries

Boundaries are sort of like rules/guidelines within a relationship to ensure everybody is comfortable, respected, and safe. Establishing and maintaining boundaries is important in any and all relationships.
