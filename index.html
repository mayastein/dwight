What is dwight?

Dwight is a website filled with tools that can help imrprove your mental health.

The site is intentionally designed with calming features, making it a safe place to turn to in moments of distress.

Exploring the site in a calm headspace is reccommended to create a concrete plan for ways to utilize the site when in crisis.

How to use this site

  • Skills: learn about skills organized by the four modules of DBT (with additional techniques from other therapies)
  • Practice: try out some skills right here on the site
  • Community: this page will soon provide a space for individuals to share resources with each other (playlists, poems, etc)
  • Crisis: this page is here to help support you through moments of high distress.
