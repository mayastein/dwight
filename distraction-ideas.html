IDEAS FOR DISTRACTION

SCROLL HERE!

Watch a calming show or movie

Create a menu or meal plan

Read a favorite book

Use the random math button below to solve random math problems

Write a letter to someone who is famous, telling them why you admire them

Use the random word button to generate ideas for things to draw.

Create a playlist for different events/moods

Get out of your space. Walk around the block, go to Target, anything to get yourself in a new location.

Give yourself a manicure or a new hairstyle

Count the number of blue things in the room. Then gray things. Then yellow. And so on.

Follow a workout video

Knit, crochet, or do any craft

Draw something or do a coloring book

Reorganize something near you (a desk, a closet, etc)

Learn to code for free on w3schools.com

Plan an event or party. Get creative with the theme and activities. Don't think about things like an invite list - this party isn't actually happening

Draw a map of your home town

See how many digits of pi you can memorize. Then do the fibbonacci sequence

Cook or bake something

Online shop (you don't need to buy anything, so completely fill your cart)

Try and draw something with your eyes closed. Or without picking up the pen. Or both

Make a dark-humor TikTok, other type of video, meme, etc. humorizing what you are going through. Don't post it until you have watched it in a calmer headspace. Just save it for now to post or delete later.

Practice your makeup skills and give yourself a crazy makeover

Go on a shoe website that lets you customize a pair of shows (Nike, Converse, etc). See what cool styles you can create

Start learning a new language on DuoLingo

Plan a luxurious vacation. Create an itinerary, including where you would stay, what you would eat, etc. Then plan vacations in different locations and with different budgets. What is the cheapest trip you can plan? Most expensive? Remember, you're not actually going on these trips!

Listen to an audibook, podcast, or music

Find a cardio dance video. Do all the moves wrong on purpose.

Find your dream house on Zillow. Then find the strangest looking house on Zillow. Then find houses for people you know.

Do oragami or learn how!

Pretend you are a reviewer for something random (types of fidgets, brands of clothes, etc). Come up with a criteria and rate the things around you.

Rip paper into very small pieces. Put the peices in a bag/bowl/some storage container for easy clean up later.

Call someone helpful or distracting

Count something - anything. Number of noodles in a box of pasta, number of kernels in a bag of popcorn, number of steps from the door to a chair, etc.

Write down every dish you can think of that has potatoes. Or that is vegetarian. Or that contains something green. Maybe challenge a friend to do the same and see who can come up with more!

Make a beaded or string bracelet. Or necklace or earings or all of the above and more

Go through your clothes and pick some that you don't wear to give away.

Play a single-player game, like solitair or 2048 online
