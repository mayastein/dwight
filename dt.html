Distress Tolerance Skills

Temperature

TIPP Skill

Check out this resource to learn about the TIPP Skill

TIPP is not the only way to cope using temperature. Here are some other tools that can help:

  1. Hold onto ice cubes/anything cold. The intense sensation is helpful in moments of crisis. Check out the distraction skills to learn more about coping skills that use intense sensations
  2. Use a cold or hot eye mask
  3. If the weather is cold, step outside without a jacket, or even just sit by an open window. Do not do this for an extended period of time

Moments of distress are TEMPORARY! Urge surfing is a skill used to delay ineffective action-urges. Keep surfing the wave until you enter a calm headspace and can evaluate the best course of action. You can learn more about urge surfing here.

Senses

Self Soothe Skill

The self soothe skill is all about using your five senses to calm your nervous sysem. Check out this resource to learn more.

Want to create your own self soothe kit? Here are some ideas for things to add!

VISION
  • print pictures of calming scenes, of your family, pets, etc.
  • create drawings with calming color schemes
  • find a small stuffed animal or figurine
HEARING
  • make a list of go-to songs
  • find a small white noise machine or radio
  • find a fidget with a calming noise (a pop it, fidget cube, etc)
SMELL
  • gather sample perfumes/air fresheners with scents you enjoy
  • find bath bombs or travel-sized soaps
  • gather scented oils, candles, etc.
TASTE
  • gather sour or sweet sucking candies and lollipops
  • add a favorite flavored beverage, like soda or bags of tea
  • find a variety of different chewing gums
TOUCH
  • add a stuffed animal, blanket, or anything with a soft texture
  • add an instant ice pack, or a heat pad
  • find calming fidgets
