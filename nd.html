Alexithymia

Many neurodivergent folks struggle with alexithymia, also known as emotional blindness, meaning we have a hard time identifying/explaining our internal experiences. One way this causes difficulties in my life is that I do not notice the early warning signs of certain emotions until they are unbearably strong, making them harder to cope with.

Check out these resources to learn more about Alexithymia and how it can be best accommodated.
